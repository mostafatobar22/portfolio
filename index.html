About

About

Mostafa is a Cairo-based Mechatronics Engineer (Ain Shams University, 2016–2021) whose career started on the shop floor — reverse-engineering molds and programming CNC machines — before moving into automotive embedded systems. Over the past 4+ years that hands-on foundation has carried through an NTI internship building an auto-parking system, R&D on electric golf cars with government partners at MATGR, and a senior role owning the OBD, fleet-management, and geofence product lines at seitech solutions across Germany and Egypt.

His strength sits at the intersection of deep protocol-level embedded work — CAN (ISO 15765), UDS, OBD-II, STM32 — and product ownership: turning client requirements into shipped, deployed software. That same bridge-building now extends into AI, as founder of REVIOM, where he's building an automotive AI agent that combines LLMs with live vehicle diagnostics and telematics.

Alongside client work, he mentors the next generation of EV engineers — guiding 43 university teams on low-voltage and powertrain design as a technical mentor for the EVER Electric Vehicle Rally — and has collaborated internationally with partners in Germany and China on EV technology transfer.
